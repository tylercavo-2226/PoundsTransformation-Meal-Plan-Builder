# Where the On-Ramp is lacking

Generated after mining everything in `_source/`. Split by who can close it, because
that is what determines how fast it moves.

---

## 1. Content gaps

| Gap | State | Who closes it |
|---|---|---|
| **Days 8-28** | 7 of 28 built. Every foundation menu at the right target has been mined dry: the 1,150 menu gave 5, the 1200cal/75g gave 3. | Cavo/RD supply more days, OR decide the week rotates 4x. **This is the single biggest open item.** |
| **Fat data** | Missing on days 1-5. That menu states calories, carb and protein only. Not derived. | Nobody, unless the RD adds it. There is no fat target, so nothing fails. |
| **Vegan track** | Not buildable. Best day assemblable from ALL their material is **4 g protein** against a 90 g floor. | Cavo: does the protein floor move for this track, or does Pounds not offer it? |
| **Dairy-free track** | Close. Every slot covered, one full day at 98 g protein. Substitutes already named by the practice (OWYN, Kite Hill, vegan powder). | RD: verify branded items. Cavo: pick the protein. |
| **Weeks 2-4 shape** | Unknown. Does the on-ramp progress week to week, or repeat one week 4x? | Cavo. See note below. |

**On weeks 2-4.** Their own `GLP CHALLENGE` runs four weeks and says "subsequent weeks
will increase the servings to maximize benefit" — but the four PDFs carry identical
guidance pages. Only the menus and shopping lists differ. The escalation is promised and
never specified. So there is no template to copy; it needs a decision.

**That challenge is also a different programme.** "NATURAL GLP" is about raising the
patient's *own* GLP-1 through food, using Akkermansia and exogenous ketones. It is not for
patients already on the medication, and its supplements are not the On-Ramp's five.
Do not merge them.

## 3. Arithmetic the RD has to rule on

Found by the build, in their own foundation menu.

- **2 meals whose calories contradict their own grams.** Baked cod states 360 kcal, its
  grams imply 268. Rotisserie bowl states 390, implies 322. Both look like a dropped fat
  digit.
- **7 places the 1200cal/75g document disagrees with itself**, including day 1 stating
  1,220 kcal when its meals sum to 1,020.

Note the contrast: the **1,150 menu reconciles perfectly on all five days.** If Cavo wants
one canonical foundation document, that is the one.
