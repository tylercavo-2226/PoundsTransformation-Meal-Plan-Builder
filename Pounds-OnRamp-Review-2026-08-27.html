What needs attention

Check

2 meals whose calories contradict their own grams

Protein ×4 plus carb ×4 plus fat ×9 should land on the stated calories. Where it does not, one of the four numbers is a typo.

  • Baked cod with green beans and sweet potato — states 360 kcal, its grams imply 268 (+92)
    fat 4g would need to be ~14g for 360 kcal to hold
  • Rotisserie chicken and black bean bowl — states 390 kcal, its grams imply 322 (+68)
    fat 10g would need to be ~18g for 390 kcal to hold
