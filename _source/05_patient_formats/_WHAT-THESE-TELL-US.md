# The practice's own patient-facing format

Two scanned worksheets Pounds already hands to patients. These are the format the
patient copy should match, not something invented.

## What they establish

**Meals are numbered, not named.** MEAL #1 through MEAL #6. Not breakfast / lunch /
dinner. Six eating occasions, and the patient fills what they use. This matches the
GLP Low Appetite Plan, which also runs on time-of-day slots rather than meal names.

**Four columns on the daily planner:**

| Column | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Meal/Snack | #1 to #6 |
| Time | The patient writes when they ate |
| Plan your meals | The meal |
| **OR** | An alternative, divided by a coral rule |

That OR column is the swap bank, already in their format. The repo's `swapGroup`
field maps straight onto it.

**The weekly diary tracks two things beyond food:** WATER and CARB TOTAL. Carbohydrate
is the number the patient is asked to total daily, which is consistent with 50-75g
being the operative constraint rather than calories.

## Design notes

- Black on **white**, not cream. These are worksheets, not brand collateral.
- Display face is a heavy brush/marker hand. Numbers and accents in coral.
- The weekly diary is **landscape**, 7 days across x 6 meals down.
- Both are scans of printed sheets, slightly skewed.

## Open question

The mark on these sheets is **#TransForm**, not the Pounds Transformation wordmark.
The social brand rules say the wordmark only, never #Transform. Patient print material
evidently uses the other mark. Confirm which belongs on the On-Ramp packet before it
is printed.
