# Variant tracks: what the practice material actually supports

Scanned every plant-based, vegan, vegetarian, dairy-free and allergen file in the dump
for usable macro data. Result is mixed, and the distinctions matter.

## The dairy-free protein substitute IS in the material

This answers "what replaces the whey". The practice already names its own substitutes in
`1200-1300 cals low dairy 110 g p .docx`:

- **OWYN dairy-free protein shake** (used as a coffee creamer, replacing oat milk)
- **Kite Hill Greek Style yogurt** (dairy-free)
- **Vegan protein powder**, mixed into the yogurt

So the plant protein does not need inventing. It needs Cavo to pick one and give a dose.

## But "low dairy" is not "dairy-free"

That menu is a REDUCTION, not an elimination. It still contains:

| Item | Day |
|---|---|
| 1/4 c fat-free feta | Breakfast 1 |
| Laughing Cow spreadable cheese wedge | Breakfast 3 |
| 1 oz mozzarella pearls | Lunch 1 and Lunch 5 |

**Not safe for a dairy allergy.** Fine for someone reducing dairy. That distinction is the
difference between a preference and an anaphylaxis risk, and the filename does not carry it.

## It also has no carbohydrate data

Every meal line reads `~300 cals/ 32 g p`. Calories and protein only. Carbohydrate is the
band Pounds actually asks patients to total daily, and this menu cannot be checked against
50-75 g. Same limitation as the 1,150 menu, but worse, because there carb was at least stated.

## Vegan: components, not menus

| Source | Macros? |
|---|---|
| `Vegan Meal Plan.pdf`, `Plant-based Meal Plan.docx`, `Vegetarian Meal Plan.docx` | **None.** Meal names only. |
| KETO Challenge plant-based weeks 1-4 | None |
| Holiday Challenge plant-based weeks | None |
| `Wt Gain-Vegetarian 2200-2300 kcals` | Full macros, but it is a WEIGHT GAIN plan at double the target |
| Branded vegan entrees (6 recipes) | **Full macros per serving** |

The six vegan entrees carry real numbers: Vegan Power Chili 230 cal, Vegan Pumpkin Chili
280, Vegan Eggroll in a Bowl 320, Eggplant Lasagna 330, Vegan Burger Meatloaf 355,
Vegan Shepherd's Pie 360. Plus dairy-free breakfast and snack recipes.

Those are **components an RD can assemble into days**. They are not assembled days, and
composing them into a diet is the RD's job, not the builder's. Worth noting the protein
problem plainly: those entrees run roughly 8-16 g protein each, and 90 g/day on 1,200
calories without dairy or whey is genuinely hard. That is a clinical problem, not a
formatting one.

## What each track needs to unblock

| Track | Blocking |
|---|---|
| **dairy-free** | A true elimination menu (not "low dairy"), carb figures, and Cavo naming the protein substitute from OWYN / Kite Hill / vegan powder. |
| **vegan** | An assembled menu at 1,200 kcal hitting 90 g protein. The components exist; the days do not. |
